Our Vision

Artistic Soulfulness

Velora is an Egyptian-based media house production founded by Ronadisa Al-Sayed.

Velora positions itself as a mission-driven photography house.

We specialize in soulful captures while adhering strictly to the principle of photographing women only with headscarves (hijab).

Every frame we create is guided by intention.
